# Scientific scope

PhysioWorld is research infrastructure for physiological modeling, information
preservation, resource-aware computation and reproducible evaluation.
The operating envelope of a model or estimator is determined by its equations,
parameters, data and context-of-use evidence.

## Information and decisions

Temporal memory is task-, sampling-, data- and model-dependent. No fixed history
window is a universal physiological memory constant. Representations can cross
in performance, and mean versus worst-task rankings can disagree. PIR is relative
to a declared task, utility, horizon, policy class, resource regime and reference;
it is not a universal scalar information measure.

Conditional means and conditional distributions answer different questions.
AUC, reconstruction error, packet delivery and task-law discrepancy are not
interchangeable estimands. Policy-optimized capability support requires
independent optimization for each feasible information condition.

## Modeling and resources

Synthetic signals and physiological worlds are model-derived. Running a model
does not validate it against people. Capacitor/Foster energy and current results
are numerical predictions under explicit model parameters, not hardware
measurements. Scalar stored energy does not in general determine deliverable
task capability; internal state, timing, safety thresholds and coupled resources
may change the ordering.

Parameter bounds and robust uncertainty sets are not statistical confidence
intervals unless a statistical construction justifies that interpretation.
Robust value uses one common policy across the uncertainty set; a minimum of
independently optimized policies is a different quantity.

## Causal and clinical evidence

Predictive transport is not causal identification. The historical fixed
orthostatic protocol does not identify general policy-optimized causal PIR.
Interventional interpretation requires temporal ordering, consistency,
exchangeability or randomization, positivity, action support and valid endpoints.
An observational replay cannot create those assumptions.

Clinical workload and frozen-protocol objects support validation studies.
Clinical benefit, diagnostic equivalence, autonomous treatment safety and
regulatory authorization require corresponding external study evidence.
Software verification is distinct from clinical validation.

## Data and reproducibility

Dataset registries describe sources and access conditions. Restricted or
credentialed raw records are user-acquired and are not redistributed. A synthetic
CI substitute verifies an adapter contract, not a human-population result.
Historical supplied figures are retained in [scientific context](scientific-context.md)
with their estimand distinctions; independent reproduction requires the original
data, protocol and estimator artifacts.
